i build small software for real businesses. the kind where the owner still picks
up the phone. lately i have been teaching robots to pick it up politely instead.

### what i am building right now

**atrio** - an ai front desk. a phone receptionist that learns a business by
reading its website, then answers calls like it has worked there for years. no
menus, no "press 2". next.js, vapi voice agents, claude api.

**outpace** - speed-to-lead. it answers a business's new leads within minutes,
in the owner's own voice, and keeps following up until a human replies. built
for trades that leak leads on nights and weekends, because the first company to
answer usually wins the job. next.js, claude, turso.

both are mine end to end: product design, unit economics, code, deploy, and the
2am when something breaks. i am currently pitching them to a local service
business with live demos instead of slides.

### the closet server

`luccaserver` is an ubuntu box in my closet. nginx, systemd, tailscale, ufw, and
one very determined little fan. everything i build eventually moves in, which
means i have broken and repaired my own dns, tls, reverse proxy and unit files
enough times to be genuinely useful with them. uptime is a collaboration between
me and the power grid.
